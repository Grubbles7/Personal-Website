< About Me />

Hi! I'm Jack McGrath, a computer technician and hardware-focused tinkerer based in Australia. I specialize in PC hardware, system troubleshooting, and building practical tech solutions from the ground up.

My journey into tech started with pulling apart machines to understand how they worked in my childhood. That curiosity evolved once i made the move to Perth and began my career in IT, that has lead to me experimenting with Linux systems, building custom PCs, homelab setups, and designing hardware projects using Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi boards.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

These days, I focus on combining hardware and software — building tools, automation systems, embedded projects, and custom 3D-printed solutions that solve real problems.
