01 / About

From the bench to the codebase

After three-plus years building and troubleshooting PC systems in Perth, I'm redirecting that problem-solving toward software. I spend my evenings and weekends building real projects in C#, ASP.NET Core, and JavaScript — not just working through tutorials.

Hardware taught me to think in systems: what's upstream, what's downstream, and where the actual fault lives versus where the symptom shows up. That's the same instinct I bring to debugging code — I just swapped a multimeter for a debugger.

I run a small homelab (five-plus machines, self-hosted services, a custom Arch/Hyprland desktop) as a place to keep learning by building — and it doubles as a proving ground for everything below.
